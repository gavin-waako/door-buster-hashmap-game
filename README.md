# Door Buster

A simple 2D multiplayer adventure set inside a large fortress made of connected rooms.
Advance through the chambers, collect items to stay alive, and survive the hazards.

## How to play

- You start in an initial room. Each room has exit doors leading to adjacent rooms.
- Rooms can hold **coins**, **food**, **both**, or a **hazard**. Some hold nothing.
- **Food** restores health. **Hazards** take it away.
- Collect the target number of **coins** in one game to win.
- You have a limited number of **steps**, set by the distance from your start to the furthest exit.
  Spend coins to buy more: **2 coins = +3 steps**.
- You're eliminated when your **health hits zero** or you **run out of steps**.
- Your score updates live on screen from your health, coins collected and food gathered.

## Controls

Arrow keys or WASD to move. Walk through a door to enter the next room.
